Custom categories · optional · decision model

Give each category a name, choose an answer format, and write a specific question. Use “Add category” for another independent judgment.

The decision model evaluates scraped text, app-store descriptions, or research summaries—not images or video. A missing detail in a research summary is not proof of absence.

Up to 12 categories. Yes/no uses a 50% cutoff; its probability is always P(yes). Choice selects one option. Include “Other” or “Unknown” when appropriate.

Click Analyze above to start the run. Download its CSV from Runs to see your labels and probabilities. If the decision model fails, standard results are kept and custom labels are blank; check Decision_Status and Decision_Error.

Definitions are remembered in this browser. After reloading the page, enable “Add your own categories” again to include them in a run.

Mixed lists are fine. Sites that block the fast crawler are retried with a rendering browser, and sites that block everything are classified from public research.
